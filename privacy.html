Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

What is collected

When you authorise Archive Scheduler with TikTok, TikTok issues an access token and a refresh token for the account @trackid.archive. Those tokens are the only data the tool receives. It does not receive your password, your followers, your messages, or any information about other TikTok users.

How it is used

The tokens are used for one purpose: uploading video files you chose into your own TikTok drafts. The tool reads nothing from your account beyond what the upload call returns about the upload itself.

Where it is stored

The tokens are stored in a file on the operator's own computer, readable only by the operator's user account. They are not copied to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. No analytics, advertising or tracking service is involved, on this page or in the tool.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator deletes the file or revokes the app under TikTok's settings for connected apps, which ends access immediately. Uploaded videos live on TikTok and are managed there.

This website

These pages are static files. They set no cookies and collect no data about visitors.

Contact

Questions about Archive Scheduler: a message to @trackid.archive on TikTok.
