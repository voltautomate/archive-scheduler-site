What this is

Archive Scheduler is a small piece of software run by the operator of the TikTok account @trackid.archive. It prepares short video clips from that operator's own library and places them in the operator's TikTok drafts through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not a public service, it has no user accounts, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Who may use it

Only the operator of @trackid.archive. If you have arrived here from a TikTok permission screen, you are the operator. No third party is granted access and no third party content is uploaded.

What it does on TikTok

With your permission it uploads video files you selected into your drafts inbox. It never publishes anything on its own. Publishing, captions, sounds and every other posting decision are made by you inside the TikTok app.

Acceptable use

The tool is used only for content the operator has the right to post. It is not used to send unsolicited messages, to interact with other accounts, to collect information about anyone, or to automate engagement of any kind.

No warranty

The software is provided as is. It may stop working when TikTok changes its API. The operator accepts that risk; nobody else relies on it.
